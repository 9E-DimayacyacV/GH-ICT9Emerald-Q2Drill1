Beginner's Guide to Baseball

Step 1

Buy a proper bat appropriate for your height. You may consult the staff at the store for which bat would be best for you.

Step 2

Master the basics. The proper stance would be having your feet shoulder-width apar, your hands squeezed tightly to the end of the bat, and rest your bat on your shoulder.

Step 3

Prioritize hitting your bat straight. The baseball should hit the center of your bat. Do not focus on strength at first, though focus on being able to hit it first.
